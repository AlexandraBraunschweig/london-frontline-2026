Who is red. Anyone aged or over, or recorded in Census 2021 as disabled enough that they cannot walk to a neighbouring street. of the district’s residents meet that test.

Who is green. Everyone else with no seat — fit to walk to a muster point, but with no car in the household and, in the baseline, no neighbour obliged to offer a lift.

Placement. Each dot sits at its household’s dwelling point, nudged within a nine-metre disc so co-residents stay countable. Dark patches are dense housing, not modelling artefacts.

Baseline definition. A household with cars seats its own people up to its own capacity, oldest and least mobile first; nobody else may board. The shared plan already refuses to send a car with no licensed driver in it — tick the driver switch to hold the baseline to the same rule.
